LCR-Reader Professional

The LCR-Reader Professional Task Kit combines everything you need for testing and troubleshooting with LCR-Reader. This task kit includes a pre-calibrated LCR-Reader and NIST Traceable Calibration Certificate, as well as a set of spare bent gold-plated tweezer probes, an extra battery and charger with micro USB cable.

Professionals previously overlooked the LCR-Reader as a useful device due to the lack of traceable calibration certificate. Siborg spent part of 2015 designing a new calibration fixture that can handle the wide range of measurement values found on newer Smart Tweezers® and LCR-Reader models. They sent the calibration fixture to Navair Technologies in Toronto, Ontario for verification that allowed Siborg to begin offering calibration certificates with LCR-Readers.

LCR-Reader Professional is available in the LCR-Reader Store, and Siborg's Amazon sales channels: Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.de, and Amazon.fr.
